## Reading (CI run 39891233946, GeneralUser GS 2.0.2, FluidSynth from Debian in swift:6.0)

- **Level (RMS)**: Swift is 5-16 dB quieter, varying by preset. Most of this is FluidSynth policy, not
  2.01: FluidSynth scales `initialAttenuation` by 0.4 (EMU hardware emulation) and adds a fixed master
  gain, while `.spec` applies the generator in full cB as 8.1.3 states. The per-preset spread follows the
  presets' attenuation generators.
- **Envelope shape**: within 2-6 dB peak-normalised (10 ms windows). Remaining differences: FluidSynth
  applies the 2.04 velocity-to-filter default modulator (not in 2.01) and uses a 64-sample envelope
  update period.
- **f0**: within 2 cents for single-layer, unfiltered patches (piano, flute, organ). Larger values
  (strings, violin, guitar, snare) come from the zero-crossing estimator on stereo / detuned layers that
  are summed to mono. They are not pitch errors: the matrix pitch rows are measured on synthetic fonts.
- **Tail**: both stop within 60 ms except pp piano C6, where 2.01 velocity attenuation leaves the tail
  under the -60 dB window sooner.
